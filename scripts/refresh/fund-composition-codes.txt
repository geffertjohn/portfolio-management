# Added Nov 2026 with the Holdings sheet. These are the DENOMINATOR for the
# top-25 holdings list: the share of the fund its top names actually carry.
# Holding COUNT is only a proxy for it and a poor one - FTZIX holds 25 names
# and VBTLX holds 16,290, but a count cannot say whether a bond index fund's
# top 25 is 4% of it or 30%. Without these the committee cannot tell a
# holdings list that describes the fund from one that describes a sliver.
